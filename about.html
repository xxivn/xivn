Hey, I'm Xivn.

I'm the person behind the little details, the late-night fixes, and the ideas that make a Minecraft server feel like more than a collection of plugins.

About me

I care about the experience on the other side of the screen.

I've spent a lot of time building, refining, and rethinking Minecraft servers. What keeps me interested isn't just getting something to work — it's making it feel right.

The first impression when a player joins. A menu that makes sense without instructions. A custom item that actually feels exciting to find. Those details might go unnoticed on their own, but together they make a world feel complete.

I enjoy bringing an idea all the way from its first sketch to something players can actually experience.
