Behind the work

From helping players to helping lead the server.

I first joined AceMC many years ago as a Helper. At the time, my job was simple: support the players, learn the community, and do what I could to make the server a better place to play.

As the years passed, my responsibilities grew along with my experience. I became more involved in the development side of the server, its presentation and rank identity, and the decisions that shape how players experience AceMC.

Today, I’m a Co-owner. It’s more than just another project in my portfolio — it’s where I got my start, and a community I’ve continued to grow alongside.
