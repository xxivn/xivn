Behind the work

The idea behind The Vale.

The Vale takes a more atmospheric approach to Minecraft survival. Instead of adding features just to have them, the goal is to make the items, economy, and player progression feel like parts of the same world. Every detail should reinforce the sense of discovery.

Warm worldbuilding, playful custom mechanics, and a long-term adventure without losing the cozy feeling of survival.
